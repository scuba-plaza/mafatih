Settings, round 2: three designs to choose between

You chose master and detail, kept Settings as a dialog over the lesson, and asked for the live preview in one section with every setting that changes it. All three designs below do exactly that. They share one set of five groups: General, Lesson, Recitation, Practice, Your data. Audio is in Recitation, “Unlock all letters” is in Practice, and Lesson holds the preview with the font, text size, vocalization, Backspace correction and keyboard guide.

They differ in two things you can mix and match afterwards: how the preview sits (pinned, slim and expandable, or in a column of its own) and how the phone navigates (push list or tabs). Everything is clickable and measured.

The three finals side by side

Each shows the Lesson section with values changed (phone) and on a desktop. Everything else is the same in all three: the five groups, the controls in each, the wording, the confirmations.

1

Push list, preview pinned on top

Phone: a list of five groups, each with its current value; tapping one opens just that group. The Lesson group holds the live preview, pinned above its controls. Desktop and tablet: the same list is a rail, the preview is pinned above the controls.

2

Push list, slim preview on phone, preview beside on desktop

Same navigation as 1. The difference is where the preview lives. Phone: one slim sample line pinned (about 60 px), tap it to open the whole preview. Desktop: three columns, the rail, the controls, and a column that never moves holding the preview, the keyboard and the keyboard guide switches that control it.

3

Tabs on phone, rail on desktop, preview in the page

Phone: no list level. Five tabs sit under the title and Settings opens straight on Lesson, with the preview at the top of the pane (it scrolls away with the page). Desktop and tablet: the rail and pinned preview of design 1.

1

Push list, preview pinned on top

Phone: a list of five groups, each with its current value; tapping one opens just that group. The Lesson group holds the live preview, pinned above its controls. Desktop and tablet: the same list is a rail, the preview is pinned above the controls.

The preview
Pinned full preview at the top of the Lesson section (the mistake behaviour is written under the sample). On a small or short screen it stops being pinned and scrolls with the controls.
Navigation
Push navigation: list, then group. Back says “Settings”. The sheet keeps one height so it never jumps between screens.
Reach
2 actions to anything (group, then control).
Tallest pane, measured
Phone (target 1.5 or less): 0.93 screens
Tablet: 0.65 screens
Desktop (target 1.0 or less): 1.38 screens
Costs
On desktop the pinned preview plus the keyboard strip leaves less room for controls, so the Lesson pane scrolls a little.

3

Tabs on phone, rail on desktop, preview in the page

Phone: no list level. Five tabs sit under the title and Settings opens straight on Lesson, with the preview at the top of the pane (it scrolls away with the page). Desktop and tablet: the rail and pinned preview of design 1.

The preview
Preview is the first block of the Lesson pane and scrolls with it, so controls get the full height once you move past it.
Navigation
Tabs: one tap to any group, always visible. Current values are not shown, and five tabs scroll sideways at 320 px with large text.
Reach
1 action to any group, 2 to a control only if it is on another tab.
Tallest pane, measured
Phone (target 1.5 or less): 1.05 screens
Tablet: 0.65 screens
Desktop (target 1.0 or less): 1.38 screens
Costs
Group values (“Scheherazade New · 40”) are not visible until you open the group. Phone and desktop navigate differently.
